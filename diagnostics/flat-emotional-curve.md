# Diagnostic: Flat Emotional Curve

Lint: **ED003**. Related: `theme-and-experience.md` emotion curve; may co-occur with BG012 endgame-drag / BG004 low-agency.

**Evidence type:** experience (P2–P4). P1 length metrics ≠ emotional intensity.

## Symptom

Intensity stays ~3/5 all game; no peak before end; "fine the whole time"; opening and end feel the same.

## Evidence to Collect

- Per-phase intensity ratings (opening / mid / pre-end / resolution) from 3+ players
- Timestamp of first "ooh" / laughter / gasp (or absence)
- Whether scoreboard drama exists without emotional peak
- Concept-brief emotion curve vs observed curve

## Diagnostic Questions

1. Is flatness whole-game or a missing mid/end spike?
2. Are stakes visible early enough?
3. Does information reveal create tension beats?
4. Is the system "solved" so peaks never appear?

## Likely Causes

| Cause | Tell |
|---|---|
| No escalation | Same decision quality turn 1 and turn 10 |
| Hidden drama | Only designer sees the close race |
| Safe optimal play | Risk never rewarded |
| End trigger soft | Game fades out |

## Candidate Interventions

| Fix | Risk |
|---|---|
| Add mid-game reveal / event beat | Randomness complaints |
| Tighten end trigger for climax | Kingmaking / rush |
| Visible race track or public threat | AP / table talk |
| One high-variance optional push | Output randomness feel |

## Minimal Experiment

**Variable:** one escalation or reveal beat in mid or pre-end.

**Metric:** Average peak intensity ≥4 in pre-end or resolution for ≥3/5 players.

**Success:** Players mark a clear "best moment" that is not setup.

## Success Criteria

Emotion curve has at least one deliberate peak; flat-at-3 across all phases is gone.
