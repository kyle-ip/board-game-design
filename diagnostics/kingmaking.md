# Diagnostic: Kingmaking

Lint: **BG006**. Related: Ch 5, `probability-and-balance.md`.

## Symptom

A player who cannot win decides who wins; last-place player throws game; spite moves dominate endgame.

## Evidence to Collect

- Final-round actions by non-contenders
- Whether elimination creates kingmaker
- Hidden vs open scoring at end
- Quotes: "I just helped X because Y blocked me"

## Diagnostic Questions

1. Can eliminated or hopeless players still affect VP totals?
2. Is there a clear 2-way race before final round?
3. Do attacks target leader only, ignoring second place?
4. Are ties common, making kingmaker pick winner?

## Likely Causes

| Cause | Tell |
|---|---|
| Player elimination | Eliminated player holds grudge cards |
| All-or-nothing area control | Last place burns leader to deny |
| Open kingmaking actions | Explicit "give VP to player" |
| No incentive to maximize own score when behind | Rational spite |

## Candidate Interventions

| Fix | Risk |
|---|---|
| Remove elimination | Longer hopeless play |
| Hide scores / limit endgame info | Opacity |
| Shared victory tiers | Dilutes solo win |
| Cap negative interaction | May reduce drama |
| Score maximization always matters | May not fix kingmaker if gap huge |

## Minimal Experiment

**Variable:** hide final-round VP track OR remove one kingmaking action.

**Metric:** kingmaking cited in ≤1/5 post-game interviews.

## Success Criteria

Hopeless players still have incentive to maximize own score in 4/5 playtests; no eliminated-player kingmaker.
