# Diagnostic: Low Memorable Moment Density

Lint: **ED004**. Related: ED003, ED007; playtesting Good-Bad-Meh.

**Evidence type:** experience (P2–P4). Win rates do not measure memorability.

## Symptom

After session, players remember almost nothing specific; "we played a game"; no stories retold; session log has no highlight reels.

## Evidence to Collect

- 24h later: can players recall one concrete moment?
- Count of spontaneous table reactions per hour
- Stories that mention other players' actions (not only own score)
- Comparable session density vs target genre (party needs more spikes)

## Diagnostic Questions

1. Are decisions reversible and low-stakes every turn?
2. Is interaction anonymous (board only) vs personal?
3. Do combos / timing windows create "I pulled it off" beats?
4. Is randomness dramatic (shared story) or opaque (shrug)?

## Likely Causes

| Cause | Tell |
|---|---|
| Smooth Euro efficiency | Optimal path, no near-misses |
| Solitaire parallel play | No collision stories |
| Weak timing | No last-second swings |
| Forgettable components | Identical turns |

## Candidate Interventions

| Fix | Risk |
|---|---|
| One public, contested timing window | AP / spite |
| Near-miss visible (almost completed set) | Frustration |
| Shared event card with table impact | Theme/randomness |
| Asymmetric one-shot ability | Balance spike |

## Minimal Experiment

**Variable:** add or heighten one moment-creating interaction.

**Metric:** ≥4/5 players recall a specific moment 24h later (prompted once).

**Success:** Debrief includes a story involving another player's action.

## Success Criteria

Memorable-moment density matches genre (party high; euro may be lower but not zero).
