# Diagnostic: Weak Player Fantasy

Lint: **ED001**. Related: `theme-and-experience.md`, Target Player Model in `design-state.md`.

**Evidence type:** experience (P2–P4 human play). Do **not** claim validated via P1 simulation.

## Symptom

Players cannot say who they are in the fiction ("I'm just moving tokens"); fantasy evaporates after teach; comparable games feel more like "being X."

## Evidence to Collect

- Exit quotes: "What was I supposed to feel like?"
- Fantasy noun named in concept-brief vs what players report
- Whether verbs on the table match the fantasy (explore vs optimize spreadsheet)
- Memorable-moment count that references theme roles

## Diagnostic Questions

1. Was a player fantasy named before mechanisms?
2. Do scoring and actions reinforce that fantasy or contradict it?
3. Is the fantasy only in flavor text / art, not in decisions?
4. Does Target Player `motivation` / `experience` match the fantasy?

## Likely Causes

| Cause | Tell |
|---|---|
| Theme coat only | Mechanisms work; fantasy never appears in play |
| Wrong aesthetic | MDA aesthetics ≠ stated fantasy |
| Abstract victory | Pure VP with no role-linked goals |
| Audience mismatch | Fantasy aims at explorers; players are optimizers |

## Candidate Interventions

| Fix | Risk |
|---|---|
| Rename actions/goals to fantasy verbs | Cosmetic if decisions unchanged |
| Add one fantasy-gated decision (role ability, story choice) | Rules bloat |
| Align scoring to fantasy success, not generic VP | Rebalance needed |
| Cut mechanisms that fight the fantasy | May remove "good" systems |

## Minimal Experiment

**Variable:** one fantasy-linked decision or scoring hook.

**Metric:** ≥3/5 players name the fantasy unprompted in debrief.

**Success:** Fantasy noun appears in Good-Bad-Meh without designer prompt.

## Success Criteria

Players describe play as inhabiting a role/verb, not only "efficient scoring."
