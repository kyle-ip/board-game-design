# Mechanism Skeleton — Orchard Run (Eval Case B)

## Core Loop

1. Pick fruit from personal orchard row
2. Deliver sets to town board for VP
3. Refresh orchard from deck

## Structure

- Turn-based, sequential (TRN-01)
- Closed economy (ECO-03)

## Candidate Comparison (locked at v0.2)

| Option | Pros | Cons | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pick-and-deliver | Simple, family-friendly | Risk of repetitive midgame | **chosen** |
| Route building | More spatial tension | Over 60 min teach | rejected |

## Rejected Alternatives

- Real-time harvest — see design-state Rejected
