```markdown
## Meta
| Experiment ID | EXP-001 |
| Status | planned |

## Objective
Test whether bid-for-start-player reduces first-player advantage.

## Hypothesis
We believe **bid-for-start-player**
will cause **seat 1 win rate ≤30% at 4p over 10 games**
because **players pay for turn order, removing free first-mover advantage**.

**Success criteria:** Seat 1 wins ≤3/10; no player reports "seat 1 always wins"
**Failure criteria:** Seat 1 still wins ≥5/10; bidding adds >5 min teach time

## Design Variable (one only)
| Variable | turn order assignment |
| Baseline | fixed clockwise |
| Variant | bid VP for start player |
| Everything else held constant | setup, actions, VP sources, card mix |
```
