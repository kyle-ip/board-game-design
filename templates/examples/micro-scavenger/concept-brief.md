# Concept Brief — Micro Scavenger (Example)

> **Format reference only** — copy granularity and section fill, not necessarily this game's design.

## Pitch
- **One sentence**: Out-scavenge your rival by chaining junk into valuable trinkets before the pile runs dry.
- **Working title**: Micro Scavenger

## Target Experience (MDA Aesthetics)

1. **Tension** — race for last useful cards
2. **Satisfaction** — small combo payoffs
3. **Mastery** — timing when to trash vs hoard

## Emotion Curve

| Phase | Intensity (1–5) | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | 3 | First grab sets tone |
| Mid | 4 | Discard pile fights |
| End | 5 | Final turn point swing |

## Theme–Mechanism Fit

Theme: post-apocalyptic junkyard scavenging. Primary verb: **collect + convert**. Fit: **Strong** with set collection (SET-*) and open discard (ECO open).

See `theme-and-experience.md`.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|

| Playtime | 10 minutes |
| Audience | family / gateway |
| Complexity target | light |

## Non-Goals
