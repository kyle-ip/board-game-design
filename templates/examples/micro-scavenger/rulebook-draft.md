# Rulebook Draft — Micro Scavenger (Example)

## Header
- **Title**: Micro Scavenger
- **Players** / **Time** / **Ages**: 2 / 10 min / 8+
- **Goal**: Most VP when the deck runs out.

## Components

- 24 Scrap cards (see components-sheet)
- VP tokens or paper track (0–20)
- Start player marker

## Setup

1. Shuffle deck; place face-down. Flip 1 card to start discard pile.
2. Each player draws 3 cards.
3. Youngest player is start player.

## How to Play

### Turn Structure

On your turn, do **one** action:

1. **Take** — Draw 1 from deck **or** take the top discard card; OR
2. **Convert** — Discard 2+ cards sharing a tag from your hand to score VP (see table).

Hand limit: 3 cards (end of turn discard down to 3).

### Scoring Sets (triangular)

| Matching tags discarded at once | VP |
|---|---|
| 2 | 1 |
| 3 | 3 |
| 4+ | 6 |

### End & Winning

When the deck is empty, finish the round so both players have equal turns. Most VP wins.

Tie-breaker: most cards still in hand ( hoarding tiebreak — example only).

## Reference

- Tags shown on each card corner icon + name
- One action per turn — no chaining

## Blind-Test Gate

- [ ] Stranger completes one full round in ≤10 min unaided (scaled for game length)
